# PIXEL QUEST: ULTIMATE RPG

A 2D pixel-art open-world action RPG that runs from a single HTML file, on desktop and mobile. There are no libraries and no external assets. Every sprite, tile, icon, sound effect and music track is generated by code, and so is the pixel font, which is built into a real TrueType font when the page loads.

## Play

- Open `dist/pixelquest.html` in a browser. It is one self-contained file.
- `dist/pixelquest-artifact.html` is the same build without the page wrapper, for hosting as a Claude Artifact.
- To rebuild after editing anything in `src/`, run `node tools/build.mjs`.

## Controls

**PC**

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrows |
| Run | Shift |
| Aim | Mouse |
| Attack (hold to charge) | Left click or J |
| Dodge roll | Space or right click |
| Interact | E |
| Ability | Q |
| Quick items | 1–4 |
| Bag | I |
| Map | M |
| Skills | C |
| Quests | L |
| Creatures | P |
| Menu | Esc |

**Touch**

- A floating joystick appears wherever your left thumb touches. Push it fully to run.
- Attack (hold to charge), Roll, a context button whose label changes (Talk, Chop, Mine, Fish, Open…), Skill, and four quick-item buttons.
- Bag, Map and Menu buttons.
- Settings can scale the buttons and mirror the layout for left-handed play.

## What's in it

- **World.** A seeded, procedurally generated overworld (224×192 tiles).
  - Six regions: Greenfields, Darkwood Forest, Sunscorch Desert, Frostfall Mountains, Volcanic Wastelands, and the Shadow Realm (a separate map).
  - Rivers, lakes, cliffs with vines to climb and ledges to hop down, towns, ruins, campfires and secret treasure.
  - Roads guarantee a route to every landmark, and a connectivity pass opens up any forest pocket you couldn't otherwise reach.
- **Region gates use mechanics, not walls.**
  - The desert opens when you deliver materials so Bram can rebuild the bridge.
  - Frostfall's cold drains your health unless you wear a Fur Cloak or stay near a fire.
  - The volcanic region sits behind a lava moat that the Glacier Charm cools as you walk.
  - The Shadow Realm opens once all four sigils are placed in the Ancient Gate.
- **Dungeons.** Six themed dungeons built from connected rooms. Each has small keys and locked doors, a crystal switch that toggles red and blue barriers to reach the boss key, a pressure-plate vault, a secret room behind a cracked wall that a bomb opens, and a boss arena.
- **Combat.**
  - **Weapons:** sword, dagger, greatsword, bow and staff all play differently. Melee weapons have combos and charged attacks, the bow draws for power shots, and the staff casts elemental bolts and charged orbs.
  - **Defense:** dodge rolls with invulnerability frames, stamina management, and critical hits.
  - **Feedback:** knockback, hit-stop, hit flashes and damage numbers.
  - **Enemy effects:** frontal shields on armoured enemies, and status effects: poison, burn, chill and freeze.
- **Enemies.** 33 enemy types across 10 behaviours: chase, hop, flyer, lunge, charge (with a wall stun), tank (frontal guard), ambush, ranged, turret, caster, area-denial and elite. Every attack is telegraphed.
- **Bosses.** Five major bosses (Forest Guardian, Sandstone Colossus, Frost Dragon, Molten Titan, Shadow Sovereign) plus the Mossback Toad King. Each has multiple attack patterns, a second phase, weak-point stuns and unique rewards.
- **Progression.** Levels and skill points, and three skill trees (Warrior, Ranger, Mage) with 15 skills, including four active abilities you can mix across trees.
- **Items.**
  - 186 items, including 75 pieces of equipment across 8 slots.
  - Six rarities, with random affixes and passive effects.
  - 10 named legendaries and mythics.
  - Item comparison, quick slots and a level requirement to equip.
- **Crafting.** 111 recipes across six stations: workbench, forge, alchemy table, cooking pot, creature altar, and your pack. You learn recipes by discovering their materials.
- **Gathering and tools.** Chop trees, mine rocks and ore, and pick herbs and mushrooms. Pickaxe tier limits which ores you can mine. Resources respawn after a few days.
- **Life skills.**
  - **Farming:** till, plant and water 8 crops that grow over days, and rain waters them for you.
  - **Fishing:** a hold-to-reel minigame with 12 fish that depend on region and time of day.
  - **Cooking:** turns crops and fish into buff meals.
- **Creatures.** 18 collectible creatures across 5 rarities.
  - Weaken one, then offer a Bond Treat.
  - Companions follow you, level up and give passive stats.
  - Each has a role: fighter, gatherer, treasure seeker, healer, guard, gardener or lantern.
  - Some evolve.
  - A journal tracks your collection.
- **People and quests.**
  - 16 NPCs with day and night routines, and 10 shops where you can buy and sell.
  - 28 quests (13 in the main story, 15 side quests) that track real actions and show markers on the minimap.
- **Home.** Bed (sleep and save), storage chest, workbench, cooking pot, and a mirror to change your appearance. You can place decorations, and an upgrade adds a forge, an alchemy table, more storage and a bigger farm.
- **Day, night and weather.** A day/night cycle with dynamic lighting from lanterns, campfires, torches and lava. Region weather: rain and storms, fog, snow and blizzards, sandstorms, falling ash and void motes.
- **Saving.** Three save slots, autosave, versioned and validated data, recovery from corrupted saves, and export/import save codes. Settings are saved separately.

## Code layout (`src/`)

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `core.js` | Namespace, math, seeded RNG, noise, storage, events |
| `font.js` | Bitmap font, canvas text renderer, runtime TrueType generator |
| `art.js` | Pixel helpers, auto-outlining, terrain tiles and transitions, world objects, item icons |
| `sprites.js` | Character, creature, enemy and boss sprite generators |
| `data.js` | All content as data: items, recipes, enemies, bosses, creatures, NPCs, shops, quests, skills |
| `world.js` | Map class, chunk rendering cache, A*, overworld, dungeon, home and Shadow Realm generators |
| `render.js` | Camera, depth-sorted drawing, lighting, minimap and world-map images |
| `fx.js` | Particles, floating text, telegraphed hazards |
| `entities.js` | Player, enemy behaviours, boss patterns, NPCs, companions, projectiles, pickups |
| `systems.js` | Time, weather, inventory, stats, crafting, quests, farming, fishing, creatures, skills, saves |
| `game.js` | Game state, interactions, combat resolution, loot, spawning, world events, persistence |
| `audio.js` | Synthesized sound effects and procedural region music |
| `input.js` | Keyboard, mouse aim, touch joystick and buttons |
| `hud.js` | Canvas HUD and the animated title scene |
| `ui.js` | HTML menus, panels, dialogue, shops, crafting, notifications |
| `main.js` | Boot, settings and the fixed-timestep main loop |

## Testing

Testing used headless Chromium driving the real game with Playwright, with no errors in any run:

- Boot, title, character creation and the first quests, played through the normal interaction code.
- Gathering, crafting, combat and loot.
- A full dungeon: keys, switch puzzle, boss door and boss fight.
- All six dungeons checked for solvability by pathfinding through their locks.
- All 33 enemy types and all six bosses, each boss through phase 2.
- Every weapon class and active skill.
- Farming over several days, fishing, creature bonding, shops and storage.
- Ledge hops, vines, swimming, cold, lava crust, bridge, gate, home upgrade, decor, NPC routines, bombing secret walls, waystones and resource respawn.
- Death, independent save slots, save round-trip, export/import, and settings persistence.
- Every screen at 1280×720 and on a phone in landscape and portrait.

Measured in headless Chromium (software rendering): about 3 ms per frame on desktop and 4 ms at phone resolution, at night with rain and 10 enemies.

**Not verified by a human:** game feel, difficulty balance and frame rate on real phones. Those need hands-on playtesting.

## Known limitations

- No shield blocking. Defense is dodge rolls with invulnerability frames.
- Touch controls can be scaled and mirrored but not freely repositioned.
- The overworld layout is generated from each save's seed, so the shape of the world differs between saves while landmarks and story stay the same.
